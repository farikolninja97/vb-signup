Know your volleyball system before playing

To join this drop-in, you must already know how to play in either a 5–1 or a 6–2 system. That includes rotations, serve-receive positioning, and knowing your responsibilities on the court.

5–1One setter

One dedicated setter runs the offense in all six rotations. There are three front-row hitters when the setter is back row, and two when the setter is front row.

6–2Two setters

Two setters rotate through the lineup. The back-row setter sets while the front-row setter attacks, keeping three front-row hitters available.

This acknowledgment is remembered on this device. Ask an organizer if you're unsure which system you should play.
