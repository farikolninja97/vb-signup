🏐 Intermediate Court Queue

The 5-1 system uses one dedicated setter who sets for all six rotations, meaning the team has three front-row hitters when the setter is in the back row, and two hitters when the setter is in the front row. The 6-2 system uses two rotating setters who only set when they are in the back row, ensuring the team always has three active hitters in the front row. Essentially, a 5-1 prioritizes the consistency of a single playmaker, while a 6-2 maximizes offensive attacking power at the net.
